About

Worked the Dock. Built the Software.

Nauti-Labs was founded by Justin LeBlanc after years inside chemical tanker operations on the Houston Ship Channel — agency desks, pre-arrival chaos, port calls that lived or died on a missing document.

The software exists because the desk needed it. Triton started as a tool to track the port calls I was working. The consulting exists because other desks kept asking how the workflow actually ran.

This isn't consulting from someone who read about shipping. It's operational knowledge from someone who stood the watch, made the calls, and chased the documents — and now builds the tools the job always needed.
